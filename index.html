Loss Penalties — carried into the next game

Safety rule: the first player to empty their hand is safe and stops playing. The game continues among the remaining players. The last player with cards loses and receives the next penalty card in this order: A♠, A♣, A♥, A♦, then K♠, K♣, K♥, K♦, continuing rank by rank down to 2♦. Penalty cards remain in their owner's playable hand in the next game. Each penalty card causes its owner to skip one dealing turn; the penalty card is counted once and is not duplicated.
